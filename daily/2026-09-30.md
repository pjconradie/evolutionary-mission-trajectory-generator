We will create a replica of the OSIRIS Rex mission. 
1) We will define a free point boundary class with initial conditions for position and velocity provided around the sun as the central body and inertial cartesian coordinates
2) the departure type is a free direct deoparture in intertial cartesian coordinates
3) arrival type is a rendezvous with chemical maneuvre

The simulation will start from low fidelity using the free point boundary ass the initial condition. the spacecraft will then do a Earth flyby, followed by an outgoing trajectory towards Bennu for rendezvous.

The mission type is a MGAnDSMs.

We interested in minimising the delta V required to reach Bennu independent of the chemical impuilse or fuel used. So it is agnostic to the rockets on the spacecraft.
